The [beam optics](/beamtracer/beam-optics/) solve traces one beamlet in 2D, through the
accelerator and the [neutralizer](/beamtracer/charge-changes/). `beamtracer.beamline` carries
the whole beam on from there, in 3D, without a mesh: the beam is a set of `Markers`, points
with a velocity, a power and a charge state, and beyond the fields the neutrals move on
straight lines.

<MethodCard name="beamtracer.beamline.Markers" headingLevel={3} />

## From the grid to the port

Each step is an example with its figures:

1. **[Grid](/beamtracer/examples/beamline-grid/).** `beamlet_markers` turns the beamlet's
   crossings of its exit plane into 3D markers (each ring of a cylindrical beamlet copied at
   random azimuths), and `ApertureGrid` copies them to every aperture of the grid, aimed as
   the grid is.
2. **[Dump](/beamtracer/examples/beamline-dump/).** `FieldRegion` pushes the ions left after
   the neutralizer through the residual-ion dump onto its plates (Boris's method in static
   fields), and the ions reionized in the duct onto its walls in the stray field.
3. **[Duct](/beamtracer/examples/beamline-duct/).** `Duct` intersects each line with the duct
   walls, simgeometry shapes, and samples where the duct gas reionizes a neutral.
4. **[Calorimeter](/beamtracer/examples/beamline-calorimeter/).** In a test facility the beam
   ends on a calorimeter: `Calorimeter` records the power on its tiles, and `fit_footprint`
   and `divergence_from_widths` infer the beam's width and divergence as the experiments do.
5. **[The whole beamline](/beamtracer/examples/beamline/)** puts the pieces in a row and adds
   up where the power went (`beamline_summary`); the neutrals at the port are written out as
   a dataset, in the tokamak's frame, for the deposition in the plasma
   ([freeofcharge](https://max-models.github.io/freeofcharge/)).

## Not in the model

- No space charge, no fields in the duct, no gas in the field regions and no wall physics.
- A reionized neutral stops where it is born: the transitions back into the neutral state are
  not followed.

## Verification

The tests compare the duct transmission and reionization with their closed forms, and the
Boris push with the Larmor circle.
