beamtracer solves steady ion beams in their own field: the extraction of positive or negative
ions from a plasma, their acceleration and focusing by electrodes and solenoids, and the
charge changes of a fast beam on a gas or a plasma. Nothing depends on time. The beam is the
fixed point of a field solve, a trace of the trajectories and a deposit of their space
charge, on a 2D mesh, planar per unit depth or cylindrically symmetric $(x, r)$.

Each equation on these pages is declared on the code that implements it, as a method card,
and checked by the tests: six closed forms against SymPy rules, seven discretisations against
their order of convergence. The pages of the section list the cards module by module; this
one shows how they fit together.

## The pipeline

<MethodCard name="beamtracer.model.BeamOptics" headingLevel={3} />

1. **[Mesh](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-mesh/).** Uniform nodes of spacing $h$; the
   electrodes are simgeometry shapes, and the distance from a node to an electrode surface
   is bracketed in 255ths of a cell.
2. **[Poisson's equation](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-poisson/)** with the Boltzmann plasma
   species, by finite differences (Shortley-Weller next to the electrodes, the axis by
   L'Hôpital) and Newton's method, second order in $h$.
3. **[Fields](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-fields/):** the electric field on the edges between
   the nodes, at the electrode surfaces taken over the bracketed distance; the magnetic field
   of solenoids or a measured profile, expanded off the axis.
4. **[Beams](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-tracer/)** seeded from an emission line with a
   quasi-random Sobol beam (the same on every iteration), traced by RK4, their charge
   $I \Delta t$ deposited at the midpoint of every step.
5. **[Charge changes](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-charge-changing/)** of every trajectory on
   the targets it crosses, as fractions (third order in the step) or as sampled events, with
   the tallies a plasma model takes its sources from.
6. **[The Vlasov iteration](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-vlasov/)** ties them together,
   relaxing the charge density until the potential stops changing:

<MethodCard name="beamtracer.solver.vlasov.vlasov_iterate" headingLevel={3} />

7. **[Beam moments](/beamtracer/numerics/solver-diagnostics/)** at diagnostic planes: rms
   emittance, Twiss parameters and the mismatch, with the trajectories of a cylindrical beam
   as rings.

Around the solve:

- The **[neutralizer](/beamtracer/numerics/neutralizer/)** iterates the traced beam with the
  global plasma model of the channel (wellbalanced) to a fixed point of the path fractions.
- The **[beamline](/beamtracer/numerics/beamline/)** carries the beam on as 3D markers on
  straight lines: a grid of beamlets, the duct with its reionization, a residual-ion dump or
  stray field (Boris push), a calorimeter and the fits the experiments make.
- The design problems, the **[einzel lens](/beamtracer/numerics/optimization-einzel/)** (with
  its thin-lens and paraxial estimates) and the
  **[H- beamlet](/beamtracer/numerics/optimization-beamlet/)**, are searched by
  **[differential evolution](/beamtracer/numerics/optimization-search/)**, which ranks the
  **[designs](/beamtracer/numerics/optimization-design/)** by feasibility first.

## Units and conventions

SI units in the solver; the configuration takes charges in e, masses in u and energies and
temperatures in eV, and converts them to C, kg and J. The beam moves along $+x$; in
cylindrical geometry $y$ is the radius $r$ and a trajectory stands for a ring, in planar
geometry a current is per metre of depth. Currents are magnitudes: the sign of the space
charge is that of the particles' charge.

## Not in the model

- No time dependence and no relativity (the normalised emittance uses $\beta$ only).
- No 3D fields in the traced region, no planar or non-axisymmetric magnetic fields.
- The source plasma is not modelled: the beams' current densities are inputs, the Boltzmann
  species has no inertia, flow or sheath, and the meniscus is where its density falls off.
- No collisions besides the charge changes (no scattering, energy loss or secondaries); the
  targets are prescribed, not transported.
- The beamline has no space charge, no fields in the duct, no gas in the field region and no
  wall physics.
- No convergence safeguards: an unconverged Vlasov iteration, Newton solve or neutralizer
  fixed point returns its last iterate, flagged but without a warning.

## Verification

Besides the checks of the cards, the tests compare the field with the coaxial and uniformly
charged cylinders, the Child-Langmuir diode with $-V (x/d)^{4/3}$, Busch's theorem through a
solenoid, the charge-state fractions with plasmacoll's on the line density, the analog mean
with the fractions mode, a parallel beam in the neutralizer with the global model, the duct
transmission and reionization with their closed forms, the Boris push with the Larmor circle,
and the thin-lens voltage of the einzel lens with a scan of the full model.
