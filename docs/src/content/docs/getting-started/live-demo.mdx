This demo runs beamtracer in your browser with [Pyodide](https://pyodide.org) (Python compiled
to WebAssembly): nothing is installed and nothing is sent to a server. Move the sliders to solve
the lens again.

Each move solves the vacuum potential of the lens of
`beamtracer.optimization.einzel.EinzelLens` on a coarse mesh (1 mm) and traces twelve rays
of a parallel beam through it, without space charge. A positive voltage decelerates the protons,
a negative one accelerates them; both focus, the decelerating lens more strongly at the same
voltage, and a voltage close to the energy stops the protons. The numbers compare the traced
rays with the thin-lens focal length and the paraxial ray of the same on-axis potential: the
outer rays cross the axis before the paraxial focus (spherical aberration), and rays wider than
the aperture are lost on the tubes.
