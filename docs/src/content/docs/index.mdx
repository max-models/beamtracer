## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Self-consistent beams" icon="rocket">
		A Vlasov iteration of field solve, trajectory tracing and space-charge deposition, in planar or cylindrical (x, r) geometry.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Extraction from a plasma" icon="sun">
		Boltzmann plasma electrons for positive ions, Boltzmann plasma ions for negative ions, which form the meniscus.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Charge changes" icon="random">
		Every trajectory carries the fractions of its particles in each charge state, on a gas or a plasma, and couples to a neutralizer model.
	</Card>
	<Card title="CPU and GPU kernels" icon="setting">
		The field gather and the charge deposit as Pyccel, NumPy, CUDA and Metal kernels through cunumpy.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
